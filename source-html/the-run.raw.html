The Mission

The Run

Goodwin Generated Mission America is a 50-state marathon project: William Goodge will run one marathon in every U.S. state in a 24-day window, beginning in Hawaii on October 9, 2026 and finishing with New York on November 1, 2026.

01

One country

The route links every U.S. state into one continuous endurance story, with each day shaped by running, transfer, recovery, and the next start line.

02

One athlete

Goodge brings proven transcontinental experience and a deeply personal purpose rooted in raising awareness for people fighting cancer.

03

One operation

Goodwin supports the attempt with aviation logistics, ground movement, live tracking, and the operational discipline needed to keep the mission moving.

How It Works

Marathon performance meets aviation precision.

Each state stop demands more than a run. The team has to protect sleep, nutrition, bodywork, media windows, crew movement, sponsor moments, and the exact transfer required to arrive ready for the next marathon.

The tracker, ticker, update archive, and weekly recap pages give followers a public view of the race as it unfolds, while the backend of the project can support Strava data, client updates, and route-specific RSVP links.
