Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected and used when you visit the Goodwin Generated Mission America website, sign up for updates, or register interest in running with Will.

Information We Collect

The site may collect information visitors voluntarily submit, including name, email address, Run With Will or RSVP information, and information submitted through site forms.

The site may also collect basic technical information needed to operate the experience, such as browser and device information, basic usage information, and local or session storage used by site features.

How We Use Information

Information may be used to send Mission updates, manage Run With Will registrations, communicate event details, operate and improve the site, and respond to inquiries.

Email Communications

Users who sign up may receive updates from Team Goodwin. Marketing communications may include an unsubscribe method provided in those messages.

Run With Will Information

RSVP information may be used to coordinate participation, timing, location, and relevant event communications.

Service Providers

Information may be processed by vendors and services used to operate the site, email communications, forms, hosting, or event logistics.

Google Sheets / Live Updates

The public Live Updates system pulls approved public update content from a Google Sheet. The sheet is used as a publishing source for update content and is not intended to expose visitor personal information publicly.

Cookies and Browser Storage

The current source uses session storage to manage the mobile intro experience and local storage for site-preview signup or comment behavior. The current source audit did not identify installed analytics cookies, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar tracking pixels.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Third-Party Links

The site links to third-party websites and services. Those external websites and services have their own privacy practices.

Your Choices

You may unsubscribe from communications using the method provided in those messages. You may also contact Team Goodwin regarding personal information at run@teamgoodwin.com.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the site, event operations, or legal requirements change. The current version will appear on this page.

Contact

Contact Team Goodwin at run@teamgoodwin.com.
