THE RECORD ATTEMPT

50 Runs. 50 States.

Hawaii to New York. October 9 to November 1. One marathon in every state, with the clock running the entire way.

THE TARGET
26.2 miles × 50 states.

Every run has to meet the requirements of the record attempt.

THE WINDOW
24 days.

From the first start in Hawaii to the final finish in New York.

THE PROOF
Every mile documented.

Route data, timestamps, tracking, media and crew documentation will create the evidence trail for the attempt.

Every stop. In order.
